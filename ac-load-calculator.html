Air Conditioning Load Calculator

Estimate the cooling load for each room in a building and get a recommended AC unit size. Add every room, set your design conditions, and see BTU/hr, kW and tons per room and for the whole building.

Note: this uses a simplified sensible/latent heat-balance method (wall & roof conduction, solar gain through glass, occupants, lighting, equipment, infiltration/ventilation) for quick sizing estimates. For code-compliant design, run a full Manual J / CLTD-CLF calculation.
