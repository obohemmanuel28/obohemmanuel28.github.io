About me

Operations strategist, powered by data.

I've spent 20+ years improving operations in the automotive, engineering, infrastructure, facilities management and energy sectors. For the last 3 years I've focused on data analytics and business intelligence to drive business transformation.

I turn transactional and operational data into decision-ready reporting for senior leadership. I work in Excel, Power BI, DAX, Power Query and SQL. I've built and automated KPI dashboards, tracked performance metrics, and made reporting more accurate, consistent and faster. I'm disciplined about cleaning, validating and structuring data, and I explain large, complex datasets in terms executives and business teams can act on.

I help organizations find operational bottlenecks, process inefficiencies and places where resources are leaking. Then I put practical fixes in place that raise productivity, strengthen governance, improve customer satisfaction and increase profit.
